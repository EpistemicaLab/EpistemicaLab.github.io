EpistemicaLab is an independent research lab. We do not build AI tools. We engineer the methods by which knowledge is produced once frontier models do most of the writing, most of the searching, and most of the reading.

Thesis.

Research capability is not a single number. It is a product of five engineerable factors. The lab's leverage lives in two of them. 

# Lab thesis
research_capability =
    question_quality     # what we ask
  × generator            # frontier model — not our edge
  × verifier             # what we trust — our edge
  × iteration_speed
  × compute

We do not compete with frontier labs on generator. We compete on question_quality and verifier, because those are the layers that AI cannot self-improve out of, yet. Our output unit is the claim, not the paper.

Programme.

Three layers of questions. Seven engineering projects. One active build.

  1. Method — how research itself becomes computable
    What is the smallest unit of research output? How does the binding constraint shift between generator quality, verifier coverage, and iteration speed? Is recursive self-improvement realistic, or does it stall on verifier saturation?
  2. Knowledge — what survives once AI floods the corpus
    What is the trajectory of AI-generated content on arXiv? When does peer-review capacity collapse? What provenance signals can a reader still trust?
  3. Risk — the failure modes of an AI-mediated science
    Monoculture (one frontier model becomes the field's blind spot). Barbell collapse (AI commodifies the middle of research careers). Training-loop contamination (AI papers train next-gen LLMs). Cognitive offloading. Coordination failure.

What this lab is, and is not.

Is
  • Method-first — verifier engineering, not model training
  • Claim-level output, not paper-count output
  • Cross-LLM audited — every load-bearing claim audited by ≥3 model families
  • Open-source code, peer-reviewed papers
  • Independent — no institutional steering
Is not
  • An AI tool company
  • An LLM wrapper
  • A coding assistant
  • A frontier capability lab
  • A vibe-coded landing page

Operating principles.

  1. Build the verifier before the generator.
    A claim with a fast, cheap verifier compounds. A claim without one rots into vibes-research.
  2. No single-LLM audits on load-bearing claims.
    One model has positive bias toward its own work. Three families across reasoning depths is the floor.
  3. Closed-set benchmarks are gameable.
    Generative tasks with hold-out sets are not. We build the second kind.
  4. If the answer changes nothing, the question is wrong.
    Zero-leverage research is the most expensive kind. We audit every project for action-relevance before it ships.
